# M02 offline acceptance — version 1.0.3+16

1. While online, sign in with your existing verified account. Open Learning
   library and confirm A1 157, A2 25, B1 30, B2 26, C1 16 and C2 15. Published
   words should not display the old review-pending banner.
2. Turn on airplane mode and leave Wi-Fi disabled. Open a level/topic, expand
   a word and check its example, translation and noun details.
3. Tap **Browse all offline vocabulary**, search an English or German term and
   clear the search. This combined browser includes 269 structured words and
   1,059 legacy entries; A1 has 495 entries. Some same-word entries are retained
   because their sources/meanings differ.
4. Return home and tap Start repeatedly. Words should change while offline,
   with no Firebase collection-load error. The current flip interaction is still
   the legacy random-card behaviour; recall scheduling belongs to M03.
5. Close and reopen the app while still offline. With the existing signed-in,
   verified account, repeat library and home checks. No cache warm-up should be
   needed for vocabulary because the files are packaged with the app.
6. Open the vocabulary quiz and check that questions load offline. Full timer,
   answer feedback and session improvements remain M04.

Restore connectivity afterwards. Report PASS/FAIL and any error text; no
screenshots or credentials are needed. First-time login/password reset and
personal-word synchronization are separate from offline vocabulary. Audio may
need a downloaded German voice. Do not sign out to test offline vocabulary.
